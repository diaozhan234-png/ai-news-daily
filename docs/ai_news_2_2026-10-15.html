Ricursive Intelligence is betting on AI-designed chips to help solve the compute crisis
Ricursive Intelligence founders Anna Goldie and Azalia Mirhoseini believe more efficient AI chip design could help solve the compute crisis. The founders took the stage at TechCrunch Disrupt on Wednesday to detail how their startup is developing AI that can design chips, learn from each iteration, and improve subsequent designs, all with the goal of automating and accelerating the chip design process. “There is this understanding that in the next five years we are going to have at least 10,000x more demand for compute, and that’s somewhat potentially conservative,” Goldie said on stage. “So we don’t want to build 10,000 times more data centers. We think that we need to help by making chips that are more efficient, that use the bandwidth in those data centers more efficiently, that use the wafers and fabs more efficiently, because even if we make [data centers] in space or something, I don’t know, you won’t see the sun anymore.” While some may be scared by the thought of AI designing its own chips, Goldie argues that letting AI handle chip design will allow humans can focus on developing applications and exploring new possibilities that can expand what they can accomplish with computing. “I think the way everything is shifting is that humans come bring their great ideas and creative thoughts, and execution is handled by AI,” Goldie said. “And this is happening in chip design, software development, and other places. And that’s something that recursive does for chip design.” The founders say AI is challenging traditional chip design methods by discovering unconventional layouts that can outperform traditional ones. For example, AlphaChip, the AI system the founders previously developed, produced a circular, “donut-shaped” arrangement of chip components that initially surprised engineers but ultimately proved to be successful. They explained that they are optimizing entire chips rather than following the traditional approach of partitioning them into dozens of smaller blocks. This approach allows AI to create intricate patterns, including wave-like designs, which the pair said they plan to share more about in the future. The founders also argued that AI-designed chips will make it possible to create all sorts of chips that might not have been possible before. “If it takes six months or a year or two years to design a new chip, then you really have to be extremely selective about the chips that you design,” Goldie said. “But if you can design a chip in like a week, and then say for manufacturing, you can design custom chips for all sorts of different types of applications in like space and AR, VR, and you biological computing, and it makes sense financially to do that, and we can be more energy efficient.” When you purchase through links in our articles, we may earn a small commission . Ricursive Intelligence正在押注人工智能设计的芯片，以帮助解决计算危机
Ricursive Intelligence创始人Anna Goldie和Azalia Mirhoseini认为，更高效的AI芯片设计可以帮助解决计算危机。周三，创始人在TechCrunch Disrupt上登台，详细介绍了他们的初创公司如何开发可以设计芯片、从每次迭代中学习并改进后续设计的人工智能，所有这些都是为了自动化和加速芯片设计过程。戈尔迪在台上说：“人们普遍认为，在未来五年内，我们对计算的需求将至少增加10000倍，这有点保守。”。“因此，我们不想建造10000倍以上的数据中心。我们认为，我们需要通过制造更高效的芯片来提供帮助，更有效地利用这些数据中心的带宽，更高效地使用晶圆和晶圆厂，因为即使我们在太空或其他地方制造[数据中心]，我也不知道，你也不会再看到太阳了。”虽然有些人可能会对人工智能设计自己的芯片的想法感到害怕，但戈尔迪认为，让人工智能处理芯片设计将使人类能够专注于开发应用程序，探索新的可能性，从而扩展他们在计算方面可以完成的工作。戈尔迪说：“我认为一切都在发生变化，人类带来了他们的伟大想法和创造性思维，而执行是由人工智能处理的。”。“这发生在芯片设计、软件开发和其他领域。这是递归对芯片设计的作用。”创始人表示，人工智能正在通过发现可以超越传统布局的非传统布局来挑战传统的芯片设计方法。例如，创始人之前开发的人工智能系统AlphaChip生产了一种圆形的“甜甜圈形状”的芯片组件排列，最初让工程师感到惊讶，但最终证明是成功的。他们解释说，他们正在优化整个芯片，而不是遵循将它们划分为数十个较小块的传统方法。这种方法允许人工智能创建复杂的图案，包括波浪形设计，两人表示他们计划在未来分享更多。创始人还认为，人工智能设计的芯片将使创造以前不可能的各种芯片成为可能。戈尔迪说：“如果设计一个新芯片需要六个月、一年或两年的时间，那么你真的必须对你设计的芯片非常有选择性。”。“但如果你能在一周内设计出一个芯片，然后说制造，你就可以为太空、AR、VR和生物计算等各种不同类型的应用设计定制芯片，这样做在经济上是有意义的，我们可以更节能。”当你通过我们文章中的链接购买时，我们可能会获得一小笔佣金。这并不影响我们的编辑独立性。Aisha是TechCrunch的消费者新闻记者。在2021年加入该出版物之前，她是MobileSyrup的电信记者。Aisha拥有多伦多大学荣誉学士学位和西部大学新闻学硕士学位。您可以通过发送电子邮件至[email protected]或通过Signal上的Aisha_malik.01加密消息联系或验证Aisha的外联活动。
