OpenAI stuck fighting Musk antitrust suit after Apple finds a way out
Elon Musk is seemingly done attacking Apple over its decision to integrate ChatGPT into iPhone features. Back in 2024, when the partnership was first announced, Musk slammed the integration as an agreement from Apple to let OpenAI install “creepy spyware” on users’ devices. The next year, he sued , claiming the partnership gave the firms a “monopoly” on Apple users’ AI prompts, which allegedly harmed competition in both smartphone and chatbot markets. For Musk, the fight with Apple seemingly escalated after he believed that his chatbot, Grok, was perhaps being illegally blocked from topping Apple’s App Store rankings. Last August, he claimed that “Apple is behaving in a manner that makes it impossible for any AI company besides OpenAI to reach #1 in the App Store, which is an unequivocal antitrust violation.” However, on Monday, Musk confirmed in a court filing that he has resolved all claims against Apple. The filing does not indicate if there were any negotiated settlement terms or otherwise explain why X would suddenly seek a voluntary dismissal. What is clear is that Musk agreed to never raise the claims again against Apple, while notably refusing to drop the same claims against OpenAI. Now, it seems that Musk plans to advance claims that OpenAI relied on the deal—which Apple emphasized was never exclusive—to monopolize the chatbot market. OpenAI has already slammed Musk’s lawsuit as another attempt to harass the AI company as Musk’s rival AI firm, now called SpaceXAI, races to catch up. Last fall, OpenAI mocked Musk’s claims as baseless , emphasizing that Musk won’t even “hazard a guess” at what portion of the chatbot market is being foreclosed by the OpenAI/Apple deal. Instead, Musk claimed it could be anywhere from 0 to 55 percent, based on “back-of-the-envelope math” that OpenAI thinks the court should reject. As the litigation proceeds, OpenAI has continued to deny allegations of antitrust violations, while seemingly moving to shore up a second line of defense. In May, OpenAI started publicly saying that it felt “burned” by the Apple integration , which OpenAI felt that Apple didn’t really promote. As one executive anonymously told Bloomberg , OpenAI expected the deal “could generate billions of dollars per year in subscriptions,” but instead Apple allegedly designed the features integrating ChatGPT in ways that made the chatbot functionality hard to discover. At that time, OpenAI was reportedly mulling legal action, as efforts to renegotiate the deal stalled. Musk’s ire for OpenAI is well-known. Earlier this year, Musk lost a lawsuit accusing OpenAI of abandoning its core mission to create AI that benefits humanity. For Musk, the loss was frustrating, since a jury unanimously ruled he waited too long to sue, not that his claims were invalid. Musk may have risked hitting a similar procedural brick wall in the antitrust litigation, as both Apple and OpenAI had emphasized that Musk does not compete in the smartphone industry and cannot pursue the claims, as none of his companies make smartphones. Previously, Apple argued that a Musk win would devastate the tech industry by setting an alarming precedent that any deal with a supplier violates antitrust law if proposals from rival firms are rejected. However, as the more narrowed litigation heads to trial this fall, it seems possible that Musk may have dropped Apple from the litigation after securing insights that he wanted. This summer, a court ordered that Apple hand over to Musk’s companies a range of internal messages in which its employees discussed the OpenAI deal. That evidence may help Musk refine his calculations on how much of the chatbot market OpenAI supposedly sought to foreclose.

在苹果找到出路后，OpenAI继续与马斯克的反垄断诉讼作斗争
埃隆·马斯克似乎已经完成了对苹果将ChatGPT集成到iPhone功能中的决定的攻击。早在2024年，当合作关系首次宣布时，马斯克就抨击了这一整合，称其为苹果公司允许OpenAI在用户设备上安装“令人毛骨悚然的间谍软件”的协议。第二年，他提起诉讼，声称这一合作关系使两家公司对苹果用户的人工智能提示“垄断”，据称这损害了智能手机和聊天机器人市场的竞争。对于马斯克来说，在他认为他的聊天机器人Grok可能被非法阻止在苹果应用商店排名中名列前茅之后，与苹果的斗争似乎升级了。去年8月，他声称“苹果的行为方式使得除了OpenAI之外的任何人工智能公司都不可能在App Store中排名第一，这无疑违反了反垄断法。”然而，周一，马斯克在一份法庭文件中证实，他已经解决了所有针对苹果的索赔。该文件没有说明是否有任何协商解决条款，也没有解释为什么X会突然寻求自愿解雇。很明显，马斯克同意不再对苹果提出索赔，同时明显拒绝放弃对OpenAI的同样索赔。现在，马斯克似乎计划进一步声称OpenAI依靠这笔交易垄断聊天机器人市场，苹果强调这笔交易从来不是排他性的。OpenAI已经抨击了马斯克的诉讼，认为这是另一次骚扰这家人工智能公司的企图，因为马斯克的竞争对手，现在名为SpaceXAI，正在竞相追赶。去年秋天，OpenAI嘲笑马斯克的说法毫无根据，强调马斯克甚至不会“冒险猜测”OpenAI/苹果交易取消了聊天机器人市场的哪一部分。相反，马斯克声称，根据OpenAI认为法院应该拒绝的“粗略计算”，这一比例可能在0到55%之间。随着诉讼的进行，OpenAI继续否认违反反垄断法的指控，同时似乎在加强第二道防线。今年5月，OpenAI开始公开表示，它对苹果的整合感到“烧伤”，OpenAI认为苹果并没有真正推动这一整合。正如一位匿名高管告诉彭博社的那样，OpenAI预计这笔交易“每年可以产生数十亿美元的订阅收入”，但据称苹果设计的功能集成了ChatGPT，使得聊天机器人的功能难以发现。据报道，当时OpenAI正在考虑采取法律行动，因为重新谈判该交易的努力停滞不前。马斯克对OpenAI的愤怒是众所周知的。今年早些时候，马斯克输掉了一场诉讼，指控OpenAI放弃了创造造福人类的人工智能的核心使命。对马斯克来说，这一损失令人沮丧，因为陪审团一致裁定他等了太久才起诉，而不是他的主张无效。马斯克可能在反垄断诉讼中遇到了类似的程序性障碍，因为苹果和OpenAI都强调，马斯克不在智能手机行业竞争，也不能追究这些索赔，因为他的公司都不生产智能手机。此前，苹果公司辩称，马斯克的胜利将开创一个令人担忧的先例，即如果竞争对手的提议被拒绝，与供应商的任何交易都将违反反垄断法，从而摧毁科技行业。然而，随着今年秋天越来越窄的诉讼进入审判阶段，马斯克似乎有可能在获得他想要的见解后将苹果从诉讼中除名。今年夏天，一家法院命令苹果向马斯克的公司移交一系列内部信息，其中苹果员工讨论了OpenAI交易。这些证据可能有助于马斯克完善他对OpenAI试图取消多少聊天机器人市场的计算。
