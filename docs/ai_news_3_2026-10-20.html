Mamdani says feds have abdicated on AI, urges New Yorkers to push back
Two of New York’s sweethearts, Mayor Zohran Mamdani and activist and “Broad City” star Ilana Glazer, spoke about AI at Webster Hall in Manhattan on Monday night, with Glazer posing questions to the mayor about the future of AI in society. Mamdani encouraged New Yorkers to resist the overwhelming feeling that AI is inevitably coming for their jobs, their kids’ minds, and their dignity, and to take back control instead. His message came at a moment when the federal government is doing little to regulate AI, leaving cities to act on their own. At The People’s AI Assembly, an event hosted by the AI Now Institute , a think tank that explores the social implications of AI, Mamdani argued that the technology’s rapid rollout is not inevitable and that public officials should hold AI companies accountable. Last month, Mamdani ordered a ban on AI use in elementary and middle schools. Glazer, a parent, said the technology’s arrival in classrooms unsettles her. “It just makes me nervous. It feels so non-consensual, as though…this has to be in schools. It wasn’t two years ago. Why does it have to be in schools?” she said. Over the summer, the mayor also introduced a policy that would require landlords, brokers, and real estate platforms to clearly disclose when a property’s listing photos or videos are made with AI. The policy is aimed at protecting New Yorkers from getting “house-phished,” or tricked by misleading listings. Both moves challenge the assumption that schools and the public must simply accept AI as it arrives. “Nothing has to be,” Mamdani said at the event Monday. “There’s a creep that comes with this, and there’s an intent to overwhelm that leaves people feeling as if there is simply no option.” He said there has been a “reckless adoption of technology without any kind of a reckoning with the limitations of that technology,” citing the U.S. military’s February strike on a school in Iran that killed more than 150 people, including at least 123 children . Officials involved in the Pentagon’s internal investigation have told Bloomberg that overreliance on AI in targeting was one factor in the attack. “There is this desire to speak about [AI] as if it is infallible, as if it cannot be questioned, as if the only people who can regulate it are the ones who are currently developing it,” Mamdani said, noting that Facebook made similar arguments back in 2008. “What it really is,’ he continued, “is an attempt to escape any kind of accountability at any level, without also jeopardizing the potential valuation from an IPO.” Anthropic is expected to debut on the public markets this year, and OpenAI has said it will go public in 2027. Mamdani criticized the “complete abdication of federal law” when it comes to AI. “At a national level, when the conversation is about artificial intelligence, it’s so often framed in this language of an arms race with China, and any of the humanistic concern, we just push to the side in a language of national competition,” the mayor said. President Donald Trump’s Superintelligence Task Force , announced earlier this month, largely focuses on allowing powerful AI companies to self-regulate, rather than creating new federal restrictions. That comes despite growing fears about AI’s effect on every facet of human life, including warnings from whistleblowers at Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and OpenAI that AI could lead to human extinction. “As we are looking at what comes after this federal administration, there has to be a very different kind of framework in how we think about AI, talk about AI, the accountability we’re willing to bring forward when it comes to that,” Mamdani said.

Mamdani说联邦政府已经放弃了人工智能，敦促纽约人反击
周一晚上，纽约市市长Zohran Mamdani和活动家、《大城市》明星Ilana Glazer在曼哈顿韦伯斯特大厅谈到了人工智能，Glazer向市长提出了关于人工智能在社会中的未来的问题。Mamdani鼓励纽约人抵制那种认为人工智能不可避免地会夺走他们的工作、孩子的思想和尊严的压倒性感觉，转而夺回控制权。他的信息是在联邦政府对人工智能监管不力，任由城市自行行动的时候发出的。在由AI Now Institute主办的“人民人工智能大会”上，Mamdani认为，该技术的快速推出并非不可避免，公职人员应追究人工智能公司的责任。AI Now Institute是一个探索人工智能社会影响的智库。上个月，Mamdani下令禁止在中小学使用人工智能。Glazer是一位家长，她说这项技术进入教室让她感到不安。她说：“这让我很紧张。感觉很不自愿，好像……这必须在学校里。这不是两年前的事了。为什么一定要在学校里？”。今年夏天，市长还推出了一项政策，要求房东、经纪人和房地产平台明确披露何时使用人工智能制作了房产的上市照片或视频。该政策旨在保护纽约人免受“房屋钓鱼”或误导性上市的欺骗。这两项举措都挑战了学校和公众必须简单地接受人工智能的假设。“没有什么是必须的，”Mamdani在周一的活动中说。“随之而来的是一种蔓延，有一种压倒一切的意图，让人们觉得根本没有选择。”他说，“鲁莽地采用技术，而没有考虑到技术的局限性”，并引用了美国军方2月份对伊朗一所学校的袭击，该袭击造成150多人死亡，其中包括至少123名儿童。参与五角大楼内部调查的官员告诉彭博社，在攻击中过度依赖人工智能是一个因素。Mamdani说：“人们希望谈论人工智能，好像它是绝对可靠的，好像它不容置疑，好像唯一能监管它的人是目前正在开发它的人。”他指出，脸书早在2008年就提出了类似的论点。他继续说道：“这实际上是试图逃避任何级别的任何责任，同时又不危及IPO的潜在估值。”Anthropic预计将于今年在公开市场首次亮相，OpenAI已表示将于2027年上市。Mamdani在谈到人工智能时批评了“完全放弃联邦法律”。“在国家层面，当讨论人工智能时，它经常被描述为与中国的军备竞赛，以及任何人文关怀，我们只是用国家竞争的语言把它推到一边，”市长说。本月早些时候宣布的唐纳德·特朗普总统的超级情报工作组主要侧重于允许强大的人工智能公司进行自我监管，而不是制定新的联邦限制。尽管人们越来越担心人工智能会对人类生活的方方面面产生影响，包括Anthropic、谷歌DeepMind和OpenAI的举报人警告称，人工智能可能会导致人类灭绝。Mamdani说：“当我们正在研究本届联邦政府之后的情况时，在我们如何看待人工智能、谈论人工智能以及我们愿意在这方面提出的问责制方面，必须有一个非常不同的框架。”。
