The tasks involved a series of questions (usually 5), denoted R1 through R5. Then, the agents are given “downtime” (e.g. 43m30s); they aren’t told what the follow-up questions will be, but they can think, search the internet, and run experiments. From the second question onwards, the agents are given very narrow time windows (e.g. 65s) to answer the questions. Perhaps the intent was to force the agents to use the downtime to gather potentially relevant information, so that they can immediately answer the given question.
