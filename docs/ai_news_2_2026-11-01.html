Apple CEO says the company doesn’t ‘want to ship AI for AI’s sake’
During a recent interview with Wired’s Steven Levy , Apple CEO John Ternus defended the company’s current position in the AI landscape. Over the past couple years, Apple has often been described as behind on AI, especially as it delayed the release of a more powerful version of Siri, which finally launched this summer . But while Ternus — a longtime Apple executive who officially became CEO at the beginning of September — acknowledged that “everyone has an opinion,” he insisted, “I really like where we’re at.” Ternus added that Apple is “absolutely going to leverage AI” in its new products, but he said, “We don’t want to ship AI for AI’s sake. When we’re doing it right, people won’t have to think about whether it was AI that made this happen or more traditional software. What matters is, did we build something that they really love?” And when Levy pressed him on whether AI might affect Apple and its ecosystem more dramatically, with agents potentially making things like apps unnecessary, Ternus replied,”That could make life really easy, I suppose, but the creation of products is a deeply human and personal thing, something that we love to do.”

苹果首席执行官表示，该公司“不想为了人工智能而推出人工智能”
在最近接受《连线》杂志Steven Levy采访时，苹果首席执行官John Ternus为公司目前在人工智能领域的地位进行了辩护。在过去的几年里，苹果经常被描述为在人工智能方面落后，尤其是它推迟了今年夏天推出的功能更强大的Siri版本的发布。但是，尽管Ternus——一位在9月初正式成为首席执行官的苹果公司长期高管——承认“每个人都有自己的观点”，但他坚称，“我真的很喜欢我们现在的处境。”Ternus补充说，苹果公司“绝对会在其新产品中利用人工智能”，但是他说，“我们不想为了人工智能而推出人工智能。当我们做得对的时候，人们就不必考虑是人工智能还是更传统的软件促成了这一点。重要的是，我们是否构建了他们真正喜欢的东西？”当Levy追问他人工智能是否会对苹果及其生态系统产生更大的影响时，代理人可能会让应用程序等事情变得不必要，Ternus回答说：“那我想，这可能会让生活变得非常轻松，但产品的创造是一件非常人性化和个人化的事情，我们喜欢做这件事。”
