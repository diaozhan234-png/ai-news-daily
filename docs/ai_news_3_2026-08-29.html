Neocloud Lambda secures $1B in debt to buy more chips
Lambda, an AI cloud company that buys computing chips and rents them out to businesses, has raised $1 billion in private, short-dated debt to buy Nvidia’s AI chips that it will lease to Microsoft, Bloomberg reports . The terms of the deal, which Bloomberg says was arranged by JP Morgan Chase, signal that Lambda is betting it will be able to quickly deploy the chips and start generating revenue from them, letting it repay the debt fairly quickly using that incoming cash. This is the latest in a string of loans that Lambda is using to fund GPU infrastructure for specific customers. In May, it closed a $1 billion secured credit facility, and this week it announced the closing of a $926 million loan to fund Nvidia GB300 GPUs, one of Nvidia’s newest chip models, for a deployment it’s under contract to provide Nvidia. The $1 billion private debt deal comes as Lambda is reportedly in talks for a $3 billion pre-IPO round . The company last November raised $1.5 billion in venture capital at a $5.43 billion post-money valuation, per PitchBook data. Lambda isn’t the only one relying on debt to fund the AI boom — according to data Bloomberg compiled, banks and tech companies have raised over $400 billion in AI-related debt globally in 2026 so far.

Neocloud Lambda获得10亿美元债务以购买更多芯片
据彭博社报道，购买计算芯片并将其出租给企业的人工智能云公司Lambda已经筹集了10亿美元的私人短期债务，用于购买Nvidia的人工智能芯片，并将其租赁给微软。彭博社称，这笔交易的条款是由摩根大通安排的，这表明Lambda押注它将能够快速部署芯片并开始从中产生收入，使其能够使用收到的现金相当快地偿还债务。这是Lambda为特定客户提供GPU基础设施资金的一系列贷款中的最新一笔。今年5月，它关闭了一项10亿美元的担保信贷，本周它宣布关闭一笔9.26亿美元的贷款，为Nvidia最新芯片型号之一的Nvidia GB300 GPU提供资金，用于根据合同向Nvidia提供部署。据报道，Lambda正在就30亿美元的首次公开募股前融资进行谈判。根据PitchBook的数据，该公司去年11月筹集了15亿美元的风险投资，融资后估值为54.3亿美元。Lambda并不是唯一一家依靠债务为人工智能热潮提供资金的公司——根据彭博社汇编的数据，到2026年为止，银行和科技公司在全球范围内筹集了超过4000亿美元的人工智能相关债务。
