Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: Fast and cost-effective with longer clips
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash brings 15-second clips, stronger consistency, and faster, more cost-effective generation. Product Manager, Google DeepMind Your browser does not support the audio element. We’re rolling out Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, which offers native 15-second video creation, improved reference adherence, and multi-turn conversational editing. This update lowers costs by 30% and provides better consistency for your professional projects. You can start using these capabilities today across the Gemini API, Google Vids, and Gemini for business to produce high-quality video content at scale. Today, we’re rolling out Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, and making it generally available across the Gemini API and Gemini for business . Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash introduces native 15-second video creation, multi-turn conversational editing capabilities, and enhanced reference following. We have dropped the price 30% from Gemini Omni 1.1 making it available at $0.07 per second for 720p video. Here’s an overview of what’s new: Beyond the lower price, the workflow keeps costs down: you can draft fast at 360p or 720p, fix a detail with a follow-up edit instead of re-generating the entire clip, and then upscale your best creations to 1080p or 4K. You can now generate and edit videos natively up to 15 seconds long, up from 10 seconds with Omni 1.1. You can also specify duration in one-second increments from 3 to 15 seconds, giving you room to tell complete stories, create product explainers, or produce full social ad spots in a single generation. You can guide generation combining character, product, or style images, short motion clips (up to three seconds), and audio cues — and Omni 1.2 Flash holds onto those details from start to finish. Faces, garments, packaging typography, and brand colors stay consistent across camera angles, making it easy to turn existing product photography or character sheets into virtual try-on clips, catalog spins, and branded video ads. Combining one model photo with front-and-back product shots of three outfits, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generates a 15-second virtual try-on sequence across 360-degree turns and match cuts while keeping the model’s identity and each garment’s cut, drape, and back details consistent. You can maintain stronger character, object, and scene consistency from the first to the last frame, with less visual drift or morphing across motion and camera transitions. Whether you’re interpolating between a first and last keyframe, or extending a clip to build a longer sequence, products, characters, props, and environments stay consistent from shot to shot. Given the same static studio lighting exhibition poster and five-layer animation prompt, Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash does not match the reference image as closely as 1.2 does. Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash builds the poster sequentially layer by layer on the dusty-pink cyclorama rising the hexagonal plinth, assembling the ribbed glass, warm filament, faceted crystal, and coiled-cord letterforms for HALO, GLOW, and LUMEN, and locking every material, shadow, and typographic detail consistently through the final frame. You can refine the same clip turn by turn — such as swapping a product or background, changing the lighting or weather, or switching the aspect ratio — instead of starting over from scratch. Multi-turn conversational editing works across full 15-second clips, making it easy to produce ad variants for different audiences and placements without re-briefing or re-generating. Starting from one generated clip, multi-turn conversational prompts swap the background turn by turn — from a striped pavilion tent to an oak forest, a stable with a white horse, a market fair, and a stone castle — while keeping both characters’ performances, armor reflections, and camera framing intact. You can generate scenes with more fluid, natural-sounding dialogue, clearer lip-sync, and distinct multi-speaker conditioning. This will show up whether you’re directing multi-character dialogue in Flow , creating video explainers in Gemini for business , or creating polished, pro videos with avatars, music and narration in Google Vids . Conditioned on one conservatory scene image alongside four character, mouth, and one-second voice references, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash routes each sampled voice to the right character with expressive lip-sync and natural comedic timing across a 15-second animated scene. You can direct complex, continuous camera movements like sweeping FPV flythroughs to dramatic zoom-outs and orbits with significantly fewer unwanted jump cuts, steadier scale, and a more polished, cinematic look. Guided by two architectural reference photos, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash directs one continuous FPV drone flight across the piazza, through the bronze doors, and up through the Pantheon’s coffered dome to the oculus with zero jump cuts. Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generates clips up to 15 seconds long that hold references together better than before. You can scroll through to see characters, text, and camera moves stay consistent across longer shots, multiple turns and transitions with no cuts. 15 second long clips: Turns one style frame into a 4-shot, 15-second macro science explainer with accurately spelled, motion-tracked callouts. Multi-turn motion transfer: Generates a rippling octopus in Turn 1, then replaces it in Turn 2 with a dancer whose gown mirrors the exact fluid motion. First-to-last frame consistency (1.1 vs. 1.2): Connects an exterior start frame and interior end frame in one continuous camera push in 1.2 Flash. You can see subtle differences like the cats on the roof, the soup ladle turning into chopsticks, and some other minor changes. Unbroken crash zoom-out: Pulls continuously from a tight shoreline close-up to an aerial island vista in one 10-second shot with natural scale and zero cuts. Businesses, startups, and creatives are already using Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash in their workflows.
