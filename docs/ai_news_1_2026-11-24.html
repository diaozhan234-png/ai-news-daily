Coherence by Construction: Synthesizing CTI Knowledge Graphs and the Formal Limits of Coherence Validation
arXiv:2611.26717v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Synthesizing relational training data such as knowledge graphs with a large language model typically emits structured objects one at a time, which keeps each object valid but leaves the relationships between them ungrounded. We study an alternative paradigm, generation by simulation: rather than describing a relational artifact, we simulate a process that produces it and take the artifact to be its causal record. Language-model agents propose intent and a deterministic referee resolves outcomes against an explicit world model, so a relation is emitted only when the event it represents occurs, making coherence a property of construction. We instantiate the paradigm for cyber threat intelligence knowledge graphs in the STIX 2.1 standard with three engines, among them an evolutionary engine applying language-model genetic operators to structured graph genomes, sharing a deterministic compiler that makes malformed and dangling output unrepresentable. We prove referential closure, causal grounding, and identifier stability, an ablation localizes each to the referee and world model rather than the policy, and two independent LLM judges reproduce the rubric's engine ranking. Our central result is cautionary, and we prove it: a validator reading any graph feature set a structure-matched random graph can reproduce is uninformative about coherence for a coherence-by-construction generator. Using Erd\H{o}s-R\'enyi controls we realize the theorem four times, as structural separation, embedding transfer, inductive link prediction, and real-data augmentation each reward a triviality rather than coherence; we give the mechanism behind each and the conditions a valid benchmark must meet.

通过构造实现连贯性：CTI知识图的综合和连贯性验证的形式极限
arXiv:2611.26717v1公告类型：new摘要：用大型语言模型合成关系训练数据，如知识图，通常一次生成一个结构化对象，这使每个对象有效，但它们之间的关系没有根据。我们研究了一种替代范式，即模拟生成：我们不是描述一个关系工件，而是模拟一个产生它的过程，并将工件作为其因果记录。语言模型代理提出意图，确定性裁判根据显式世界模型解析结果，因此只有当它所代表的事件发生时才会发出关系，使连贯性成为构造的一个属性。我们在STIX 2.1标准中用三个引擎实例化了网络威胁情报知识图的范式，其中一个是进化引擎，它将语言模型遗传算子应用于结构化图基因组，共享一个确定性编译器，使格式错误和悬空的输出无法表示。我们证明了引用闭包、因果基础和标识符稳定性，消融将每个都定位到裁判和世界模型，而不是政策，两名独立的LLM裁判再现了量规的引擎排名。我们的中心结果是警示性的，我们证明了这一点：验证器读取结构匹配随机图可以再现的任何图特征集，对于构造生成器的一致性来说，并不了解一致性。使用Erd\H{o}s-R\enyi控制我们实现了四次定理，即结构分离、嵌入转移、归纳链接预测和真实数据增强，每一次都奖励琐碎而不是连贯；我们给出了每个基准背后的机制和有效基准必须满足的条件。
