Don't Blame The Preferences: Examining The Emergence of Sycophancy in LLM Training
arXiv:2611.10045v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: AI sycophancy, the tendency of large language models (LLMs) to flatter users and affirm their beliefs, can undermine accuracy and contribute to misinformation, dependence, and overconfidence. Although prior work has benchmarked sycophancy in released models and proposed mitigations, its origins during training remain poorly understood. Preference alignment methods such as RLHF and DPO are often blamed, but their effects are rarely compared with earlier training stages. We investigate sycophancy across the training pipelines of three model families and three benchmarks. We find that sycophancy is largely established by the end of supervised fine-tuning (SFT), rather than introduced by preference alignment. Across all nine model benchmark combinations, SFT checkpoints attain at least 92% of preference aligned models' sycophancy scores and at least 85% of final models' scores; on average, they are about as sycophantic as final models. Preference alignment increases sycophancy in only four of nine cases, by an average of 4.2 percentage points when it does. To probe the underlying causes, we isolate model representations associated with sycophancy using mechanistic interpretability and measure their activation on training datasets from each stage. SFT datasets show roughly the same average activation as other training data but contribute substantially more examples, and the highest-scoring individual datasets all come from SFT. Our findings suggest that addressing sycophancy requires interventions throughout the training pipeline, particularly in data selection and augmentation for pre-training and SFT.

不要责怪偏好：检查LLM培训中Sychophancy的出现
arXiv:2611.10045v1公告类型：new摘要：人工智能阿谀奉承，即大型语言模型（LLM）倾向于奉承用户并肯定他们的信念，会破坏准确性，导致错误信息、依赖和过度自信。尽管之前的工作已经在发布的模型和提出的缓解措施中对阿谀奉承进行了基准测试，但其在训练过程中的起源仍然知之甚少。RLHF和DPO等偏好对齐方法经常受到指责，但它们的效果很少与早期训练阶段进行比较。我们调查了三个模型家族和三个基准的培训管道中的阿谀奉承行为。我们发现，阿谀奉承在很大程度上是由监督微调（SFT）的结束建立的，而不是由偏好对齐引入的。在所有九种模型基准组合中，SFT检查点至少达到偏好一致模型的阿谀奉承得分的92%和最终模型得分的85%；平均而言，他们和最终的模特一样阿谀奉承。在九种情况中，只有四种情况下偏好一致会增加阿谀奉承，平均增加4.2个百分点。为了探究根本原因，我们使用机械可解释性来分离与阿谀奉承相关的模型表示，并在每个阶段的训练数据集上测量它们的激活情况。SFT数据集显示与其他训练数据大致相同的平均激活率，但贡献了更多的例子，得分最高的个体数据集都来自SFT。我们的研究结果表明，解决阿谀奉承问题需要在整个培训过程中进行干预，特别是在预培训和SFT的数据选择和增强方面。
