Who May Overrule the Agent? Evidence-Gated Authority in LLM Review Institutions
arXiv:2611.05390v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Multi-agent language-model systems often add a reviewer to improve an upstream agent's decision. Yet a reviewer can both repair errors and replace decisions that were already correct. We study this as a problem of institutional jurisdiction: conditional on the same proposal, task information, model, and review output, when is the reviewer authorized to make its recommendation binding? We compare broad override authority with an evidence gate that permits replacement only after the reviewer produces a mechanically valid record of a binding-constraint violation and a compliant repair. Across two locally run open-weight models, 96 spatial-policy tasks, and a 96-task portfolio-transfer benchmark, the gate sharply reduces corruption of correct proposals while retaining most repairs of violations. The directions of this tradeoff are partly implied by the rule; its magnitude depends on reviewer transitions and upstream proposal states. A prospective validation then gives the reviewer an explicit choice to retain, replace, or escalate. This eliminates correct-proposal corruption for Qwen, but Mistral still replaces all 48 correct proposals; Qwen also declines every welfare-only repair that Mistral frequently attempts. Thus interface design changes reviewer behavior but is not a model-independent safeguard. The result is not that restricted review is universally better. It is that reviewer competence, action interface, and binding jurisdiction jointly determine both the correction domain and the corruption surface.

谁可以凌驾于代理人之上？法学硕士审查机构的证据门控权威
arXiv:2611.05390v1公告类型：new摘要：多代理语言模型系统通常会添加一个审阅者来改进上游代理的决策。然而，审阅者既可以修复错误，也可以替换已经正确的决定。我们将此作为一个机构管辖权问题进行研究：在相同的提案、任务信息、模型和审查输出的条件下，审查员何时有权使其建议具有约束力？我们将广泛的覆盖权限与证据门进行了比较，证据门只允许在审查人员出示违反约束和合规修复的机械有效记录后进行更换。在两个本地运行的开放权重模型、96个空间政策任务和96个任务组合转移基准中，该关卡大大减少了正确提案的腐败，同时保留了对违规行为的大部分修复。该规则部分暗示了这种权衡的方向；其规模取决于审阅者转换和上游提案状态。然后，前瞻性验证为审阅者提供了保留、替换或升级的明确选择。这消除了Qwen的正确提案腐败，但Mistral仍然替换了所有48个正确提案；Qwen还拒绝了Mistral经常尝试的每一项仅限福利的修复。因此，界面设计改变了审阅者的行为，但并不是一种独立于模型的保护措施。结果并不是限制性审查普遍更好。正是审查员的能力、行动界面和约束管辖权共同决定了纠正领域和腐败表面。
