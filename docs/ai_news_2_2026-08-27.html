AI agents meant to replace Meta workers made “large-scale, disruptive actions”
Earlier this year, Meta created a “plan” to reduce some of its teams by as much as 60 percent to make the company “AI native,” Reuters reported today, citing two people familiar with Meta’s internal affairs. Reuters’ report highlights the challenges organizations face when analyzing the best uses for AI and determining when the technology is a better fit for certain tasks than employees. Meta confirmed to Reuters that the plan, reportedly codenamed Project OT (short for organization transformation), explored scenarios in which Meta reduced some team headcounts by 60 percent and that the plan called for two rounds of layoffs. Meta wouldn’t confirm which teams Project OT affected. As part of our company restructuring earlier this year, we asked some teams to conduct a scenario planning exercise looking at the potential impact of redeployments, open role closures and cuts. This ultimately resulted in moving thousands of employees to do priority work on several newly-established teams, as has been publicly reported. Ultimately, we didn’t move forward with every scenario from the exercise – and it was never assumed we would. Reuters reported that Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg “set Project OT in motion and directed executives to proceed with the changes to management structures” earlier this year. It said that the first round of layoffs from Project OT occurred in May and that Meta canceled the second. Reuters said it reviewed “scores of internal documents, posts, and recordings” and spoke with “more than 20 people with knowledge of Meta’s inner workings” about the since-scrapped plans. Per the publication, Meta executives created Project OT in January. Project OT reportedly explored using AI to perform “much of the daily work performed by thousands of human employees.” Small teams of people would oversee the AI, the report said, citing “one internal planning document reviewed by Reuters and three people familiar with the project.” In these scenarios, Meta would give some employees new roles and lay off others. One HR executive reportedly said that Meta would have reduced its headcount by about 25 percent or more under these scenarios. Meta told Reuters that it canceled Project OT before determining how many layoffs the plan called for. Meta was going to use some of the money that it saved from layoffs to pay for high-performing employees, especially those with AI engineering skills, Reuters reported, citing “two people familiar with the matter.” One of the Project OT documents that Reuters reviewed described “AI native” as a company where “AI-ready tools and agents interact, workflows are automated, [and] new builds are AI-first.” Being “AI native” also means, per the document, selling AI agents to third parties, which Meta started doing in June. This year, Meta reportedly launched a pilot program that restructured engineering teams, research teams, and at least eight other teams into smaller groups. This followed the reported October publishing of an internal post called “AI-Native Playbook,” in which a Meta product management team member outlined how the pilot would remove middle management and use “agent-assisted analysis” to help prioritize daily tasks. Reuters noted that HR employees and “AI systems” would help Meta’s leaders decide about promotions; however, Meta told the publication: “Performance rating and promotion decisions were and are made by people, not AI.” Immediately after issuing the May layoffs, Zuckerberg reportedly canceled the November wave. Reuters said it “was unable to determine what exactly prompted Zuckerberg to shift course.” Reuters said March and April reports of impending layoffs and Meta tracking employees’ keyboard and mouse input in order to train AI agents (Meta has since paused the program ) hurt employee morale. Another potential factor in Meta’s decision to scale back Project OT was uncertainty about whether increased AI use would boost productivity. Citing internal posts, Reuters noted: “For instance, code changes made to the internal software platforms and infrastructure employees use on the job were up 220 percent year-over-year, according to a post by [Meta CTO Andrew] Bosworth in early June. But changes that led to new or upgraded features reaching Meta users were only up 36 percent.” Internal posts also reportedly pointed to AI agents making “large-scale, disruptive actions that humans are unlikely to execute” and leading to a 40 percent increase in major technical and security incidents compared to the prior year. Employee time spent resolving those problems increased by as much as 70 percent. Meta declined to comment on these internal posts. In July, however, Zuckerberg reportedly acknowledged during a company meeting that the “trajectory of the agentic development over at least the last four months hasn’t really accelerated in the way that we expected.” Many organizations, including those much bigger and much smaller than Meta, are exploring ways that AI could help with company goals, such as by saving employees time, driving revenue, or reducing costs, including, in some cases, human resources. Meta’s story also shows how far some firms are willing to go to implement AI in an advantageous way, even at the cost of jobs . Meta’s case suggests that even some of the most eager organizations may struggle to replace various human workloads with AI while also highlighting the risks of overzealous AI projects.

旨在取代Meta员工的人工智能代理做出了“大规模、破坏性的行动”
路透社今天援引两位熟悉Meta内部事务的人士的话报道称，今年早些时候，Meta制定了一项“计划”，将其部分团队减少多达60%，使公司成为“人工智能原生”。路透社的报告强调了组织在分析人工智能的最佳用途以及确定该技术何时比员工更适合某些任务时面临的挑战。Meta向路透社证实，据报道，该计划代号为Project OT（组织转型的缩写），探讨了Meta将一些团队人数减少60%的情况，该计划要求进行两轮裁员。Meta不会确认Project OT影响了哪些团队。作为今年早些时候公司重组的一部分，我们要求一些团队进行情景规划，研究重新部署、开放角色关闭和裁员的潜在影响。正如公开报道的那样，这最终导致数千名员工在几个新成立的团队中优先工作。最终，我们并没有推进演习中的每一个场景，也从未想过我们会这样做。路透社报道称，Meta首席执行官马克·扎克伯格今年早些时候“启动了OT项目，并指示高管们继续进行管理结构的变革”。它说，Project OT的第一轮裁员发生在5月，Meta取消了第二轮裁员。路透社表示，它审查了“数十份内部文件、帖子和录音”，并与“20多名了解Meta内部运作的人”就废弃的计划进行了交谈。根据该出版物，Meta高管在1月份创建了Project OT。据报道，Project OT探索了使用人工智能来完成“数千名员工所做的大部分日常工作”。该报告援引“路透社审查的一份内部规划文件和三名熟悉该项目的人”的话说，将由一小群人监督人工智能。在这些情况下，Meta将给一些员工新的角色，并解雇其他员工。据报道，一位人力资源高管表示，在这种情况下，Meta将减少约25%或更多的员工。Meta告诉路透社，在确定该计划要求裁员多少之前，它取消了OT项目。路透社援引“两位知情人士”的话报道称，Meta将用裁员节省下来的一部分资金来支付高绩效员工的工资，尤其是那些具有人工智能工程技能的员工。路透社审查的一份OT项目文件将“人工智能原生”描述为一家“人工智能就绪的工具和代理交互，工作流程自动化，[和]新构建是人工智能优先”的公司。根据该文件，“人工智能本地”还意味着向第三方出售人工智能代理，Meta于6月开始这样做。据报道，今年Meta启动了一项试点计划，将工程团队、研究团队和至少八个其他团队重组为更小的团队。此前，据报道，10月份发布了一篇名为“AI Native Playbook”的内部帖子，其中一名Meta产品管理团队成员概述了试点将如何删除中层管理人员，并使用“代理辅助分析”来帮助确定日常任务的优先顺序。路透社指出，人力资源员工和“人工智能系统”将帮助Meta的领导者决定晋升；然而，Meta告诉该出版物：“绩效评级和晋升决定过去和现在都是由人做出的，而不是人工智能。”据报道，在发布5月份的裁员通知后，扎克伯格立即取消了11月份的裁员计划。路透社表示，“无法确定究竟是什么促使扎克伯格改变方向。”路透社表示，3月和4月关于即将裁员的报道以及Meta跟踪员工键盘和鼠标输入以培训人工智能代理的报道（Meta已暂停该计划）伤害了员工士气。Meta决定缩减OT项目的另一个潜在因素是，不确定增加人工智能的使用是否会提高生产力。路透社援引内部帖子指出：“例如，根据[Meta CTO Andrew]Bosworth 6月初的一篇帖子，员工在工作中使用的内部软件平台和基础设施的代码更改同比增长了220%。但导致Meta用户获得新功能或升级功能的更改仅增长了36%。”据报道，内部帖子还指出，人工智能代理做出了“人类不太可能执行的大规模破坏性行为”，导致重大技术和安全事件比上一年增加了40%。员工花在解决这些问题上的时间增加了70%。Meta拒绝对这些内部帖子发表评论。然而，据报道，扎克伯格在7月份的一次公司会议上承认，“至少在过去四个月里，代理开发的轨迹并没有像我们预期的那样真正加速。”许多组织，包括那些比Meta大得多和小得多的组织，都在探索人工智能可以帮助实现公司目标的方法，例如节省员工时间、增加收入或降低成本，在某些情况下还包括人力资源。Meta的故事还表明，一些公司愿意以有利的方式实施人工智能，即使是以牺牲就业为代价。Meta的案例表明，即使是一些最渴望的组织也可能难以用人工智能取代各种人类工作负载，同时也突显了过度热衷的人工智能项目的风险。
