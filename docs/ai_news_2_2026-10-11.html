Microsoft’s Satya Nadella says AI models need an ‘emergency brake’
Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella is the latest tech executive to offer lengthy thoughts on how AI safety might be improved. In a Saturday morning post on X , Nadella wrote that it’s time “to step back and assess the trust architecture” of AI. “We can’t treat Super Intelligence as a set of nested black boxes and simply accept or reject its recommendations, answers, and actions,” Nadella wrote, using the Trump administration’s preferred term for AI . As outlined by Nadella, this approach “means separating the model from the harness that orchestrates its work,” as well as “externalizing controls and safeguards.” He also called for “every meaningful model action” to be documented with “tamper-proof human readable evidence,” and for systems where “an authorized person” always has the ability “to pause or shut down a model mid-task.” “We must assume a model is compromised and contain it from the start,” he said. “Think of it like an emergency brake.” Nadella’s comments come as leading AI companies acknowledge more and more incidents where they seemed to lose control of their models , and after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a plan for more cautious AI development.

微软的萨蒂亚·纳德拉表示，人工智能模型需要“紧急刹车”
微软首席执行官萨蒂亚·纳德拉是最新一位就如何提高人工智能安全性发表长篇思考的科技高管。纳德拉在周六早上的一篇X帖子中写道，是时候“退一步，评估人工智能的信任架构”了。“我们不能把超级智能视为一组嵌套的黑匣子，简单地接受或拒绝它的建议、答案和行动，”纳德拉写道，他使用了特朗普政府对人工智能的首选术语。正如纳德拉所概述的那样，这种方法“意味着将模型与协调其工作的工具分开”，以及“将控制和保障措施外部化”。他还呼吁用“防篡改的人类可读证据”记录“每一个有意义的模型行动”，并呼吁“授权人员”始终有能力“在任务中期暂停或关闭模型”的系统。“我们必须假设模型受到损害，并从一开始就加以控制，”他说。“把它想象成一个紧急刹车。”纳德拉发表此番言论之际，领先的人工智能公司承认越来越多的事件，他们似乎失去了对模型的控制，在Anthropic首席执行官Dario Amodei发布了一项更加谨慎的人工智能开发计划之后。
