Closing the Analyze-to-Design Gap: An Authorization-Bounded Agent for Self-Driving Laboratories
arXiv:2611.25043v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI) agents are increasingly integrated into self-driving laboratories (SDLs) to plan experiments and interpret results. However, analytical reasoning alone does not make a laboratory autonomous: autonomy in an SDL is achievable only when the agent can be trusted to act on physical hardware unsupervised and knows when to defer to a human. Here, we present an architecture that integrates an agent at the Analyze-Design interface of the Design-Make-Test-Analyze (DMTA) cycle, enabling agent-driven measurement selection and data interpretation while ensuring that state-changing operations are authorized, validated, and auditable. We demonstrate this architecture on a liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry (LC-MS) instrument in an oxidant-screening campaign for synthesizing an organic dye. Within human-defined campaign limits, the agent submitted measurement jobs, interpreted mass spectrometry (MS) and diode-array detection (DAD) data, flagged failed experiments, and produced an oxidant ranking. This ranking matched that obtained from scripted peak integration, and organic chemists confirmed the agent's spectral interpretations and failure assignments. In a simulated environment that replays the archived LC-MS data, where the agent chose which wells to inject under varying injection limits, it measured the highest-yielding condition at about its seventh injection, against 14.5 on average for a random order, and identified it in every campaign with a limit of eight or more injections. An agent can therefore close the analysis-to-design gap while its actions stay bounded and verifiable, which is a step toward greater autonomy for SDLs.

缩小分析到设计的差距：一种用于自动驾驶实验室的授权有界代理
arXiv:2611.25043v1公告类型：new摘要：人工智能（AI）代理越来越多地集成到自动驾驶实验室（SDL）中，以计划实验和解释结果。然而，仅靠分析推理并不能使实验室自主：只有当代理可以在无人监督的情况下对物理硬件进行操作，并且知道何时服从人类时，SDL中的自主性才能实现。在这里，我们提出了一种架构，该架构在设计-制造-测试-分析（DMTA）循环的分析-设计界面集成了一个代理，实现了代理驱动的测量选择和数据解释，同时确保状态更改操作得到授权、验证和可审计。我们在合成有机染料的氧化剂筛选活动中，在液相色谱-质谱（LC-MS）仪器上演示了这种架构。在人类定义的活动范围内，该试剂提交了测量作业，解释了质谱（MS）和二极管阵列检测（DAD）数据，标记了失败的实验，并产生了氧化剂排名。这一排名与脚本化峰积分的排名相匹配，有机化学家证实了该试剂的光谱解释和故障分配。在回放存档LC-MS数据的模拟环境中，试剂选择在不同的注射限制下注射哪些井，在大约第七次注射时测量最高产量条件，而随机顺序的平均产量为14.5，并在每次注射限制为八次或更多次的活动中对其进行识别。因此，代理可以在其行为保持有界和可验证的同时缩小分析与设计之间的差距，这是SDL实现更大自主权的一步。
