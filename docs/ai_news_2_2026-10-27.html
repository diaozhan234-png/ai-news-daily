Apple releases iOS and macOS 27.2 with new Siri AI features, new Music logo
Today, Apple rolled out the 27.2 updates for its operating systems, including iOS, macOS, watchOS, tvOS, visionOS, and so on. Let’s start with iOS 27.2. This update is heavily focused on Siri AI. It adds a memory feature to Siri, so it can “recall your past conversations” and reference them when chatting with you. It also expands what data sources Siri can access when giving responses to include voicemails, Messages attachments, and more. On the flip side, there is now an “Excluded Apps” option that allows you to “choose which apps Siri, Search, and suggestions can access.” Further, Siri AI is now available in five more languages beyond English, as previously announced: French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish—though Siri AI remains unavailable in Europe (and mainland China). Other additions include context-aware suggestions within Messages (like turning a message inviting you to something into a calendar entry), SynthID watermarking support in Photos, and support for user profiles in the Apple TV app. There’s also the new Apple Music branding , plus the arrival of previously promised Apple Pencil support on the iPhone Duo foldable. The macOS Golden Gate 27.2 offers some of the same things: contextual memory for Siri, the same additional Siri AI languages, and miscellaneous unspecified bug fixes. The same goes for the Apple Watch—again, mostly the same Siri-related updates. visionOS 27.2 includes the new languages and updated Music icon, as well. Also, as previously discovered in the beta releases of macOS 27.2, there are some references to new gesture recognition capabilities in applications like Photos, Notes, and Safari—which may be related to unconfirmed reports of touch support on the next MacBook Pro refresh, which may be revealed at a scheduled public briefing this Wednesday. Apple’s own iOS and macOS update notes are below. Siri AI (All iPhone 16 models and later, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max) This update provides enhancements to Siri AI with the ability to retain useful context from previous Siri interactions as well as draw from a wider range of personal context. It also introduces Suggestions in Messages that help you take quick actions in apps like Calendar and Reminders based on context from your conversation. This release includes additional features, bug fixes, and security updates for your Mac.

苹果发布iOS和macOS 27.2，配备新的Siri AI功能和新的音乐标志
今天，苹果推出了其操作系统的27.2更新，包括iOS、macOS、watchOS、tvOS、visionOS等。让我们从iOS 27.2开始。此次更新主要关注Siri AI。它为Siri添加了记忆功能，这样它就可以“回忆起你过去的对话”，并在与你聊天时引用它们。它还扩展了Siri在回复时可以访问的数据源，包括语音邮件、消息附件等。另一方面，现在有一个“排除的应用程序”选项，允许您“选择Siri、搜索和建议可以访问哪些应用程序”。此外，Siri AI现在除了英语外，还有五种语言可供选择：法语、日语、韩语、葡萄牙语和西班牙语，尽管Siri AI在欧洲（和中国大陆）仍然不可用。其他新增功能包括Messages中的上下文感知建议（例如将邀请您参加某项活动的消息转换为日历条目）、Photos中的SynthID水印支持以及Apple TV应用程序中对用户配置文件的支持。还有新的Apple Music品牌，以及之前承诺的iPhone Duo可折叠手机支持Apple Pencil。macOS Golden Gate 27.2提供了一些相同的东西：Siri的上下文记忆、相同的额外Siri AI语言以及各种未指明的错误修复。Apple Watch也是如此，主要是与Siri相关的更新。visionOS 27.2还包括新的语言和更新的音乐图标。此外，正如之前在macOS 27.2的测试版中发现的那样，照片、笔记和Safari等应用程序中都提到了新的手势识别功能，这可能与下一次MacBook Pro更新时未经证实的触摸支持报告有关，该报告可能会在本周三的例行公开发布会上公布。苹果自己的iOS和macOS更新说明如下。Siri AI（适用于所有iPhone 16及更高版本、iPhone 15 Pro和iPhone 15 Pro Max）此更新增强了Siri AI的功能，能够保留以前Siri交互中的有用上下文，并从更广泛的个人上下文中提取信息。它还引入了消息中的建议，帮助您根据对话中的上下文在日历和提醒等应用程序中快速采取行动。此版本包括Mac的其他功能、错误修复和安全更新。
