OpenAI's first branded hardware is... a light-up keyboard?
As rumors continue to swirl about OpenAI’s work on a personalized smart speaker and other hardware, the company is today rolling out its first branded device. The $230 Codex Micro is a specialized, RGB-lit mini-keyboard designed to let users monitor and quickly interact with multiple Codex agents with a glance and a few clicks. The device is described as a “limited-run collaboration” with Work Louder , which already sells a very similar-looking Creator Micro line of customizable square keyboards targeted at creative professionals. The Codex Micro differentiates itself from those mainly through six frosted keys in the top two rows, which offer color-coded live feedback on up to six Codex threads, even when they are not in focus on-screen. Ideally, those colored keys will cycle from white when a thread is idle to blue when Codex is thinking to green when a task is complete. But the keys can also flash amber when Codex requires feedback or a decision from a human operator and red when a thread encounters an error, letting users know at a glance which of their Codex tasks needs immediate attention. A quick tap on the lit-up button brings up the applicable Codex window on-screen. That kind of simplified, always-available thread monitoring could be handy for users who keep a half-open laptop to monitor their continually running AI agents. But this kind of physical keyboard attachment is, of course, primarily useful in a desktop setting, while ChatGPT’s mobile app can offer more detailed monitoring away from the desk. Below those frosted light-up buttons, the Codex Micro offers six buttons mapped by default to common Codex tasks like accepting and rejecting changes, branching threads, and a “push to talk” button for audio prompts. But those functions can be remapped through software and altered physically with one of 32 included keycaps. And while the first “layer” of customized functions is reserved for Codex, users can program and cycle through five other function sets for general computing shortcuts. While the Codex Micro is at heart simply a slightly modified version of existing third-party hardware, it features prominent OpenAI branding on the box and on the face of the device itself (along with a “You can just build things” tagline referencing OpenAI’s Super Bowl ad ). The “limited run” partnership is also being described as accepting orders only “while supplies last,” suggesting that long-term availability is still up in the air (a representative did not respond to questions about total availability as of press time). Still, the launch of the first OpenAI-branded hardware highlights the company’s ambitions to expand beyond LLM-based software. Those ambitions have been apparent since at least 2023 , when OpenAI reportedly started collaborating with former Apple design chief Jony Ive. Last May, OpenAI acquired Ive’s design firm LoveFrom , a partnership reportedly focused on a handheld, screenless device that accepts audio and visual input from around the user. Last October, the Financial Times reported that the collaborative design effort had run into unexpected technical and design hurdles that could delay the device’s reported plans for a 2026 launch. And just last week, Apple threw a potential wrench into OpenAI’s hardware plans with a lawsuit alleging the theft by an ex-Apple employee of trade secrets related to hardware manufacturing . The Codex Micro is taking orders now and expects to ship to users “shortly after purchase,” according to a spokesperson.

OpenAI的第一个品牌硬件是…一个发光键盘？
随着有关OpenAI在个性化智能扬声器和其他硬件方面的工作的传言不断，该公司今天推出了第一款品牌设备。售价230美元的Codex Micro是一款专门的RGB发光迷你键盘，旨在让用户通过一瞥和几次点击即可监控多个Codex代理并快速与之交互。该设备被描述为与Work Louder的“有限运行合作”，后者已经销售了一款外观非常相似的Creator Micro系列可定制方形键盘，面向创意专业人士。Codex Micro主要通过顶部两行的六个磨砂键与这些键区分开来，即使它们在屏幕上没有聚焦，也能为多达六个Codex线程提供颜色编码的实时反馈。理想情况下，这些彩色键将从线程空闲时的白色循环到Codex正在思考时的蓝色，再到任务完成时的绿色。但是，当Codex需要人类操作员的反馈或决定时，按键也会闪烁琥珀色，当线程遇到错误时，按键会闪烁红色，让用户一目了然地知道哪些Codex任务需要立即关注。快速点击亮起的按钮，屏幕上将显示适用的Codex窗口。这种简化的、始终可用的线程监控对于那些半开笔记本电脑监控其持续运行的AI代理的用户来说可能很方便。当然，这种物理键盘附件主要在桌面环境中有用，而ChatGPT的移动应用程序可以在桌面之外提供更详细的监控。在这些磨砂亮起按钮下方，Codex Micro提供了六个按钮，默认情况下映射到常见的Codex任务，如接受和拒绝更改、分支线程和用于音频提示的“一键通”按钮。但这些功能可以通过软件重新映射，并使用32个附带的键帽之一进行物理更改。虽然定制功能的第一层是为Codex保留的，但用户可以对其他五个功能集进行编程和循环，以实现通用计算快捷方式。虽然Codex Micro本质上只是现有第三方硬件的轻微修改版本，但它在盒子和设备本身的表面上都有突出的OpenAI品牌（以及引用OpenAI超级碗广告的“你可以构建东西”标语）。“有限运行”伙伴关系也被描述为“在供应最后的时候”只接受订单，这表明长期可用性仍悬而未决（截至发稿时，一位代表没有回答有关总可用性的问题）。尽管如此，首款OpenAI品牌硬件的推出突显了该公司超越基于LLM的软件的雄心壮志。至少从2023年开始，这些雄心壮志就很明显了，据报道，OpenAI开始与前苹果设计总监Jony Ive合作。去年5月，OpenAI收购了Ive的设计公司LoveFrom，据报道，该公司专注于一种手持式无屏幕设备，可以接受用户周围的音频和视频输入。去年10月，英国《金融时报》报道称，合作设计工作遇到了意想不到的技术和设计障碍，这可能会推迟该设备2026年发布的计划。就在上周，苹果公司提起诉讼，指控一名前苹果员工窃取了与硬件制造有关的商业机密，这可能会对OpenAI的硬件计划造成影响。一位发言人表示，Codex Micro现在正在接受订单，预计“购买后不久”将发货给用户。
