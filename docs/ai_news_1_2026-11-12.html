EarthRecord: From Pixels to Land Change with Multimodal Agents over Four Decades of Satellite Imagery
arXiv:2611.12934v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: While multimodal agents now carry out complex data analysis with tools, their ability to turn decades of satellite imagery into reliable land-change analysis, crucial for Earth observation science, remains unmeasured. Existing benchmarks fall short: they pose questions on curated images or give agents preprocessed inputs, leaving out the raw multi-sensor archives where such analysis begins. We introduce EarthRecord, a benchmark built on the raw record of 56 sites: four decades of Landsat, MODIS, VIIRS, and Sentinel-2 data, with cloud masking and cross-sensor harmonization left to the agent. It poses 238 land-change questions across ten analysis tasks, scored against withheld references from global products, agreement among methods, and visual interpretation. Two controls test what answers rest on: the closed book removes the record, and instrument pairs alter one satellite's data while nothing changes on the ground. Evaluations of twelve multimodal agents reveal that leading agents complete most analyses end to end but falter when an answer requires mapping from imagery. Further analysis shows that the record moves answers from prior knowledge to the pixels but not yet to the real change on the ground: when one satellite's data are altered, half of the right answers follow the alteration. EarthRecord thus provides a principled platform for measuring and advancing agents from pixels to land change.

地球记录：从像素到陆地变化——四十年卫星图像的多模态代理
arXiv:2611.12934v1宣布类型：新摘要：虽然多模态代理现在使用工具进行复杂的数据分析，但它们将数十年的卫星图像转化为可靠的土地变化分析的能力，这对地球观测科学至关重要，仍然无法衡量。现有的基准不足：它们对精心策划的图像提出了问题，或者给代理提供了预处理的输入，遗漏了开始此类分析的原始多传感器档案。我们介绍了EarthRecord，这是一个基于56个站点的原始记录的基准：40年的Landsat、MODIS、VIIRS和Sentinel-2数据，云遮蔽和跨传感器协调留给代理。它在10个分析任务中提出了238个土地变化问题，根据全球产品中保留的参考、方法之间的一致性和视觉解释进行评分。两个控制测试了答案的依据：合上书删除记录，仪器对改变一颗卫星的数据，而地面上没有任何变化。对12个多模态代理的评估表明，主要代理端到端完成了大多数分析，但当答案需要从图像中映射时，它们会犹豫不决。进一步的分析表明，该记录将答案从先验知识转移到像素，但尚未转移到地面的实际变化：当一颗卫星的数据被更改时，一半的正确答案会随之更改。因此，EarthRecord为测量和推进从像素到土地变化的因素提供了一个有原则的平台。
