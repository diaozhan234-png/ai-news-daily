Gemini 4 Carbon: Our next frontier model
Gemini 4 Carbon is our strongest model with state-of-the-art cybersecurity and long horizon capabilities. SVP, Google DeepMind and Chief AI Architect, Google Today, we’re announcing the next frontier model: Gemini 4 Carbon, our strongest model with state-of-the-art cybersecurity and long horizon capabilities. Carbon ranks #1 on the recently updated v5.0 of Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59. It shows significant gains across coding and agentic tasks as well as across key capabilities critical for cybersecurity defense, landing a firm lead in SEC-bench Pro (88.2%) for finding vulnerabilities, ExploitGym (54.8%) for exploitation, and CWE-bench (71.0%) for patching. On internal benchmarks, Carbon drives a strong uplift in generating secure code, and in live coding environments with our own teams it shows reduced misalignment and fabrication rates. Building on Gemini 4 Argon , which was launched two weeks ago, Carbon illustrates the pace at which we’re pushing the frontier forward. Our researchers and models are working hand in hand to advance capabilities faster, while strengthening our safeguards in tandem. We're taking a deliberate approach to rollout and are actively engaged in the U.S. government’s voluntary process for pre-release model testing. Starting this week, we will make Carbon available to a subset of cyber defenders and will gradually phase access to additional trusted partners through our Fairwind Program . We’re continuing to gather feedback and iterate on guardrails before making Carbon available to developers, businesses, and consumers via our product surfaces as soon as possible. Gemini 4 Carbon will launch at the same introductory price 1 as Argon at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input tokens priced at 95% off input token price. Designed to take on much harder, long-horizon engineering problems, Carbon is built for sustained agentic workflows. Internally, our teams have seen Carbon run for days to persist through dead ends to tackle complex, multi-step engineering tasks like root-causing and patching subtle bugs across large codebases and low-level systems. Building on user feedback, we focused on improving model performance across a variety of domains, including coding, agentic, and scientific tasks, as well as full-stack web development and multimodal understanding. With improved quality of output and artifacts in web development, Carbon jumps +89 elo points to land at #4 (1768) on WebDev Arena . Gemini 4 Carbon delivers a significant leap in cybersecurity capabilities. It achieves state-of-the-art performance across the cybersecurity defense lifecycle — vulnerability detection, validation, and remediation. Our teams are already seeing strong gains in cyberdefense. On internal benchmarks, Carbon achieves more than 2x uplift in success rate for generating secure code over Argon. As another example, the Chrome team has reported that Carbon performed more than 10X better than another lab's large frontier model when generating high quality patches. Wiz, our leading Cloud security platform, also observes Carbon leading on benchmarks for web penetration testing, a core capability for Wiz's Scan for Good initiative. Carbon excels across key external benchmarks (evaluated without guardrails): Gemini 4 Carbon builds on the same multi-layered safety and security stack of Argon — combining model training, real-time misuse defenses, prompt-injection resilience, and chain-of-thought monitoring. In particular, we made significant strides in model alignment with Argon, and reported its propensities to behave in an aligned way in our Safety and Security report . With Carbon, we challenged ourselves to go a step further on alignment, and trained the model to respond to complex or high-pressure tasks in the right way. Pushing the frontier forward requires moving fast while remaining deliberate about deployment. With Gemini 4 Carbon, our goal is to deliver meaningful leaps in software engineering and cybersecurity defense without compromising on safety or reliability. We’re eager to see how the initial cohort of cyber defenders use this model and we look forward to bringing Carbon to developers, businesses, and consumers soon. After the introductory period expires, the price of $4 per 1M input tokens and $20 per 1M output tokens will apply.
