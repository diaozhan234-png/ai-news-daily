Anthropic’s annualized revenue surges to $65B
Anthropic’s revenue continues to not only grow at an historic pace but also to accelerate. The model maker’s annualized revenue run rate — a projection of a full year’s revenue based on a recent, shorter period —surpassed $65 billion at the end of July, Bloomberg reported on Monday, up from $47 billion in May and just $9 billion at the end of last year. Anthropic didn’t immediately respond to our request for comment. The company’s investors expect it to continue to grow at approximately the same rate for the remainder of the year, finishing 2026 between $100 billion and $120 billion , the Financial Times reported. Meanwhile, rival OpenAI has doubled its revenue to $40 billion, up from $20 billion at the end of 2025, Bloomberg reported last week . The two companies may calculate their revenue metrics differently, but Anthropic’s growth rate has captivated investors far more than OpenAI’s has. Both companies have filed confidential IPO paperwork. Anthropic is expected to hit the public markets ahead of OpenAI — possibly as soon as this fall. Anthropic will be seeking a public valuation of $2 trillion or more, according to the Financial Times, which would make it the largest market debut on record. Anthropic was last valued at $965 billion in late May, when it raised a $65 billion round.

Anthropic的年收入激增至650亿美元
Anthropic的收入不仅继续以历史性的速度增长，而且还在加速增长。彭博社周一报道，该模型制造商的年化收入运行率（基于最近较短时期的全年收入预测）在7月底超过650亿美元，高于5月份的470亿美元和去年年底的90亿美元。Anthropic没有立即回应我们的置评请求。据英国《金融时报》报道，该公司的投资者预计，在今年剩余时间里，该公司将继续以大致相同的速度增长，到2026年将达到1000亿至1200亿美元。与此同时，彭博社上周报道称，竞争对手OpenAI的收入从2025年底的200亿美元翻了一番，达到400亿美元。这两家公司可能会以不同的方式计算收入指标，但Anthropic的增长率比OpenAI更吸引投资者。两家公司都提交了机密的IPO文件。预计Anthropic将在OpenAI之前上市——可能最早在今年秋天。据英国《金融时报》报道，Anthropic将寻求2万亿美元或更高的公开估值，这将使其成为有史以来最大的市场首次亮相。Anthropic上一次估值为9650亿美元是在5月底，当时它筹集了650亿美元的资金。
