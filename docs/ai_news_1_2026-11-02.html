OpenAI might rebrand its data centers as ‘AI compute campuses’
Data centers are deeply unpopular . But what if we called them something else? Axios reports that OpenAI is preparing a “PR blitz” for after the midterm elections on Tuesday, as Democrats seem likely to win back the House of Representatives, and possibly the Senate, too . The company’s chief global affairs officer Chris Lehane told Axios that the midterms will be “a bit of a movie trailer for what you’re going to see in 2028,” when he expects AI to be “ the issue” in the presidential election. So OpenAI is to influence the AI debate moving forward, with Lehane discussing several proposals, including the development of AI “speed limits” ; finding ways to help people see AI as empowering them in their daily lives; pushing the U.S. to take a role in developing global standards — and yes, rebranding data centers as “AI compute campuses.” Lehane also described the conversation around data centers (a debate that has pushed Amazon and Microsoft to say they will no longer use NDAs in their negotiations with local governments ) as “good and healthy.” “Communities should hold companies responsible and accountable so they really and truly get a transformative benefit from these projects,” he said.

OpenAI可能会将其数据中心重新命名为“人工智能计算园区”
数据中心非常不受欢迎。但如果我们叫他们别的呢？Axios报道称，OpenAI正在为周二中期选举后的“公关闪电战”做准备，因为民主党人似乎有可能赢回众议院，也可能赢回参议院。该公司首席全球事务官Chris Lehane告诉Axios，中期选举将是“2028年你将看到的电影预告片”，届时他预计人工智能将成为总统选举中的“问题”。因此，OpenAI将影响未来的人工智能辩论，Lehane将讨论几项提案，包括人工智能“速度限制”的发展；寻找方法帮助人们将人工智能视为在日常生活中赋予他们力量；推动美国在制定全球标准方面发挥作用，是的，将数据中心重新命名为“人工智能计算园区”。Lehane还将围绕数据中心的对话（这场辩论促使亚马逊和微软表示，他们将不再在与地方政府的谈判中使用新授权）描述为“良好和健康的”。“社区应该让公司负责和问责，这样他们才能真正从这些项目中获得变革性的利益，”他说。
