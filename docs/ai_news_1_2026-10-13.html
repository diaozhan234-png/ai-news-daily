Faithfulness Without Anchors: A Position Paper on the Structural Misalignments of Explainable AI Faithfulness Metrics for Feature Importance
arXiv:2610.12493v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) tools have often been presented as methods to increase trust in machine learning models by making aspects of their algorithms more human-understandable. However, evidence accumulated over the last decade points to an overall failure of this objective, instead promoting overreliance on incorrect predictions, which can subsequently lead to algorithmic aversion. Rather, XAI appears to function primarily as a debugging tool, useful for obtaining anecdotal insights into, for example, features learned by the model. One of the main issues underlying this limitation is that XAI methods often oversimplify the inner workings of the model, resulting in misalignment between the explanation and the model itself - a behavior referred to as unfaithfulness or infidelity. Faithfulness can be interpreted as the degree of approximation between an explanation and the model. Computing faithfulness in a rigorous manner would thus make it possible to quantify one of the various elements of trustworthiness of an explanation. However, existing metrics present significant limitations, making this quantification either infeasible or computationally expensive. In this position paper, we outline five major shortcomings of current faithfulness metrics and propose four possible avenues for research to bolster their reliability.

无锚的可信度：关于特征重要性的可解释AI可信度度量的结构错位的立场文件
arXiv:2610.12493v1公告类型：new摘要：可解释人工智能（XAI）工具通常被视为通过使其算法的各个方面更易于人类理解来提高对机器学习模型信任的方法。然而，过去十年积累的证据表明，这一目标总体上失败了，反而助长了对错误预测的过度依赖，这可能会导致算法厌恶。相反，XAI似乎主要用作调试工具，可用于获取对模型学习到的特征的轶事见解。这种局限性背后的主要问题之一是，XAI方法经常过于简化模型的内部运作，导致解释与模型本身之间的不一致——这种行为被称为不忠或不忠。可信度可以解释为解释和模型之间的近似程度。因此，以严格的方式计算可信度将使量化解释可信度的各种要素之一成为可能。然而，现有的指标存在重大局限性，使得这种量化要么不可行，要么计算成本高昂。在这份立场文件中，我们概述了当前忠诚度指标的五个主要缺点，并提出了四种可能的研究途径来提高其可靠性。
