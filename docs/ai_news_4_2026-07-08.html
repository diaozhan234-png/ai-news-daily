Facing US export controls, China's DeepSeek plans to make its own chips
DeepSeek, the Chinese startup developing large language models that are competitive with those from US companies like OpenAI and Anthropic, is planning to enter the silicon business, according to Reuters . Citing three people familiar with the matter, Reuters writes that DeepSeek has been working on a move into silicon for about a year. It has been meeting with potential partners in the hardware and silicon space and has been hiring engineers for the project. The focus is on data center chips for inference, not training, and the goal is likely to reduce reliance on both Huawei and Nvidia. Nvidia is the chipmaker for most AI companies in North America and Europe, but a United States export ban has prevented the company from achieving a similar presence in China. Huawei controls about half of the data center chip market there, and DeepSeek isn’t the only one trying to enter; Chinese tech giants like Alibaba and Baidu have been making moves, too. While chip export controls in the US are a major reason this is an urgent concern for DeepSeek, US-based AI companies are making similar chip plans. For example, OpenAI and Broadcom jointly announced Jalapeño , the former’s first chip designed for inference at scale, just a couple of weeks ago. Anthropic, too, has been exploring custom chip design, though there have not been any publicly visible milestones yet. In OpenAI’s case, it’s partly a play to reduce its reliance on Nvidia, but it’s also a desire to have Apple-like control over the entire tech stack for its products. Further, getting in at the silicon and data center levels can be an advantage in a market where data center access is likely to remain constrained, with multiple companies competing for compute as they scale up their AI models and services.

面对美国的出口管制，中国的DeepSeek计划生产自己的芯片
据路透社报道，中国初创公司DeepSeek正在开发与OpenAI和Anthropic等美国公司竞争的大型语言模型，并计划进入硅业务。路透社援引三位知情人士的话说，DeepSeek已经致力于进军硅领域大约一年了。它一直在与硬件和硅领域的潜在合作伙伴会面，并一直在为该项目招聘工程师。重点是用于推理的数据中心芯片，而不是训练，目标可能是减少对华为和英伟达的依赖。英伟达是北美和欧洲大多数人工智能公司的芯片制造商，但美国的出口禁令阻碍了该公司在中国实现类似的业务。华为控制着那里大约一半的数据中心芯片市场，DeepSeek并不是唯一一个试图进入的公司；阿里巴巴和百度等中国科技巨头也在采取行动。虽然美国的芯片出口管制是DeepSeek迫切关注的一个主要原因，但总部位于美国的人工智能公司也在制定类似的芯片计划。例如，就在几周前，OpenAI和博通联合宣布了Jalapeño，这是前者第一款用于大规模推理的芯片。Anthropic也一直在探索定制芯片设计，尽管目前还没有任何公开可见的里程碑。在OpenAI的案例中，这在一定程度上是为了减少对Nvidia的依赖，但也是希望苹果能够控制其产品的整个技术栈。此外，在数据中心访问可能仍然受到限制的市场中，进入硅和数据中心层面可能是一个优势，因为多家公司在扩大其人工智能模型和服务的同时竞争计算。
