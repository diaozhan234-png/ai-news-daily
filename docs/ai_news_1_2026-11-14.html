Together Compiler: Continual Compilation for AI Inference
arXiv:2611.14681v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Today's AI workloads are rapidly changing: new models are released more often, model architectures are increasingly diverse, and traffic distributions change frequently. The challenge we face at Together AI is continual optimization for these changing workloads. Compiling a deployment into an optimized forward pass is a challenge that is well-suited for agent hill-climbing. Existing systems struggle with continual compilation due to a tradeoff between measurement throughput and fidelity. Many systems use agents to optimize individual kernels from the inference forward pass using offline benchmarks, but timings are low-fidelity because of data-dependent behavior in primitives like mixture-of-experts (MoE). On the other hand, systems use agents to directly optimize end-to-end engines but suffer from slow engine launch times. To address these challenges, we build Together Compiler (TKCC), the first truly continual agent loop designed for AI inference optimization. To address the throughput-fidelity tradeoff, we propose: (1) a Tiered Persistent Engine that decouples engine launch overheads from forward pass computation; (2) a cost model to select the parallelism configurations that seed the optimization process. We also conduct extensive ablations on agent context management and present our insights on deploying TKCC in production. TKCC outperforms highly-optimized hand-written Megakernels on the Llama 1B workhorse workload by up to 1.7x and the best open source engines on the latest frontier models (Kimi K3, GLM 5.3, MiniMax M3.1 Flash, DeepSeek v4.1 Flash) across hardware platforms by up to 2.6x higher TPS on the InferenceX benchmark. TKCC also sets the state-of-the-art performance at batch size 1, with 1111 TPS on M3.1 Flash and 1205 TPS on DeepSeek v4.1 Flash. Finally, TKCC generalizes beyond frontier language model inference. TKCC serves trillions of tokens across our production fleet.

Together编译器：人工智能推理的连续编译
arXiv:2611.14681v1公告类型：new摘要：当今的人工智能工作负载正在迅速变化：新模型的发布越来越频繁，模型架构越来越多样化，流量分布也经常变化。我们在Together AI面临的挑战是不断优化这些不断变化的工作负载。将部署编译为优化的前向传递是一个挑战，非常适合代理爬山。由于测量吞吐量和保真度之间的权衡，现有系统在持续编译方面遇到了困难。许多系统使用代理通过离线基准从推理前向传递中优化单个内核，但由于混合专家（MoE）等原语中的数据依赖行为，时间保真度较低。另一方面，系统使用代理直接优化端到端引擎，但引擎启动时间较慢。为了应对这些挑战，我们构建了Together Compiler（TKCC），这是第一个真正为AI推理优化设计的连续代理循环。为了解决吞吐量保真度的权衡问题，我们提出：（1）一个分层持久引擎，将引擎启动开销与前向传递计算解耦；（2）成本模型，用于选择为优化过程播下种子的并行配置。我们还对代理上下文管理进行了广泛的消融，并介绍了我们对在生产中部署TKCC的见解。TKCC在Llama 1B主力工作负载上的性能比高度优化的手写Megakernels高出1.7倍，在InferenceX基准测试中，跨硬件平台的最新前沿型号（Kimi K3、GLM 5.3、MiniMax M3.1 Flash、DeepSeek v4.1 Flash）上的最佳开源引擎的TPS高出2.6倍。TKCC还将最先进的性能设置为批量1，M3.1闪存上的性能为1111 TPS，DeepSeek v4.1闪存上为1205 TPS。最后，TKCC超越了边界语言模型推理。TKCC为我们的生产团队提供数万亿个代币。
