Taiwan’s chipmaking giant TSMC to invest $1B in Anthropic, OpenAI
Top chip manufacturer TSMC is ready to invest $1 billion in Anthropic and OpenAI. That would give the Taiwanese chipmaker direct stakes in leading AI developers, even as its own contract chipmaking business has profited enormously from the AI data center buildout. The chipmaker’s board approved plans to invest up to $500 million in OpenAI preferred stock reserved for large investors and another $500 million to purchase Anthropic common shares at the latter’s expected IPO price, according to Reuters . Such an investment may give markets “a shot of confidence as the artificial-intelligence trade shows signs of strain,” The Wall Street Journal reported. But analysts also warned that such circular financing among investors, suppliers, and customers has made it difficult to evaluate real market demand for AI-related hardware and services, according to the Journal. TSMC’s chip fabrication plants physically produce the vast majority of Nvidia’s chips designed for AI data center workloads and make most of Apple’s custom chips used in iPhones , Mac laptops and desktops, plus Apple’s planned AI computing server . Most of these fabs are located in Taiwan, but TSMC also operates fabs in Japan and China, and it has an expanding US manufacturing cluster in Arizona. It is also building a new fab in Germany. Meanwhile, Anthropic and OpenAI are both purchasing huge quantities of AI hardware manufactured by TSMC for their data center projects. So TSMC’s announced investment certainly seems to fit into the broader trend of circular financing that has become common during the AI boom, even as tech stocks have gyrated recently as investors have grown increasingly jittery. Still, TSMC has plenty of money to spare on such a bet. Ongoing demand for AI-related chips has enabled the Taiwanese chipmaker to report six consecutive quarters of record profit, with the latest example being the company earning more than $23 billion in profit during the July through September 2026 quarter, according to Reuters . TSMC’s planned investment in Anthropic and OpenAI also pales in comparison with circular financing deals from major US tech companies. The most notable example is Nvidia , which has invested $30 billion in OpenAI and $10 billion in Anthropic so far, in addition to putting tens of billions of dollars into many other AI and robotics companies .

台湾芯片制造巨头台积电将向Anthropic、OpenAI投资10亿美元
顶级芯片制造商台积电准备向Anthropic和OpenAI投资10亿美元。这将使这家台湾芯片制造商直接入股领先的人工智能开发商，尽管其自己的合同芯片制造业务从人工智能数据中心的建设中获得了巨大的利润。据路透社报道，这家芯片制造商的董事会批准了向OpenAI优先股投资高达5亿美元的计划，该优先股是为大型投资者保留的，另外还有5亿美元以Anthropic的预期IPO价格购买其普通股。据《华尔街日报》报道，这样的投资可能会给市场“一剂信心，因为人工智能行业显示出紧张的迹象”。但据《华尔街日报》报道，分析师也警告称，投资者、供应商和客户之间的这种循环融资使得评估人工智能相关硬件和服务的实际市场需求变得困难。台积电的芯片制造厂实际生产了英伟达为AI数据中心工作负载设计的绝大多数芯片，并生产了苹果用于iPhone、Mac笔记本电脑和台式机的大部分定制芯片，以及苹果计划中的AI计算服务器。这些晶圆厂大多位于台湾，但台积电也在日本和中国经营晶圆厂，并在亚利桑那州拥有一个不断扩大的美国制造集群。它还在德国建造一座新的晶圆厂。与此同时，Anthropic和OpenAI都在为其数据中心项目购买大量台积电制造的人工智能硬件。因此，台积电宣布的投资似乎确实符合人工智能繁荣时期普遍存在的循环融资的更广泛趋势，尽管随着投资者越来越紧张，科技股最近出现了波动。尽管如此，台积电在这样的赌注上仍有充足的资金。据路透社报道，对人工智能相关芯片的持续需求使这家台湾芯片制造商能够连续六个季度报告创纪录的利润，最新的例子是该公司在2026年7月至9月的季度盈利超过230亿美元。与美国主要科技公司的循环融资交易相比，台积电计划对Anthropic和OpenAI的投资也相形见绌。最引人注目的例子是Nvidia，到目前为止，该公司已向OpenAI投资300亿美元，向Anthropic投资100亿美元，此外还向许多其他人工智能和机器人公司投资了数百亿美元。
